# Policy Discovery Record

Capture at minimum:

- authority mode and who established it;
- repository visibility and license/source boundary;
- applicable instruction files and their scope;
- primary remote, base branches, protected branches, and PR route;
- repository, CI, framework, and deployment-identity adapters;
- per-owner, per-worktree, and per-lane active-change limits;
- capability declarations for read, edit, push, merge, deploy, delete, and public disclosure;
- evidence TTL and stale-state invalidation triggers;
- high-risk paths and business domains;
- native type, lint, unit, integration, migration, build, browser, and live checks;
- environments and which actions can mutate each one;
- merge, deploy, monitoring, cleanup, and publication authority.

Treat missing information as unknown, not permission.
